Welcome to ReGuess

ReGuess is a puzzle game.
The goal is to correctly guess a 5-letters word in 5 attemps.
Before starting to play we strongly suggest having a look at our rules to avoid any misunderstandings while playing the game.
If there are still questions after reading the rules concerning the game feel free to contact us by pressing the Contact button on the top right of this page.
Enjoy the game!
